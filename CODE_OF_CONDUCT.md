# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and clear. Welcome contributors with different
backgrounds and experience. Critique code and evidence without personal attacks.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing and unwanted sexual attention are
unacceptable in project spaces.

Maintainer [alexanderisgood](https://github.com/alexanderisgood) handles moderation.
Responses may include a request to edit content, removal of content or restrictions
on participation. Decisions should explain the relevant behavior and response.
You may ask the maintainer to reconsider a decision without repeating private or
abusive material publicly.

For abuse on GitHub, use the platform's
[reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or [report-abuse form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Keep private information out of public issues and pull requests.
